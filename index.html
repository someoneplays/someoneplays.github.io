Let’s Play A Game

At the end of the day… it’s tomorrow.

Let’s Play A Game is an offline party and relationship game experience designed for couples, friends, families, and groups who want to laugh, connect, and start conversations.

Relationship Lab

15 games built for couples.

Getting To Know You

Open-ended prompts where you build a personal checklist of answers. Type whatever comes to mind, save each entry, and share it with your partner at the review card.

Do You Even Know Me

Rank all four options in the order you think your partner prefers them. The second player then marks each pick correct or incorrect to reveal who really knows whom.

Heart Check

Each prompt uses a scale from 1 to 10 so you can rate exactly how strongly you feel. There are no wrong answers—just honest ones.

Are They A Red Flag

Type a short answer to each scenario, then decide whether your own response feels like a green flag or a red flag. It is more revealing than it sounds.

Love Language Quiz

Explore real relationship situations, choose one answer, and optionally explain why it fits you.

Deal Breakers

Type a short response to each scenario, then decide whether your answer feels like a deal or a deal breaker for you.

Setting Boundaries

Read the boundary subject, then type your own boundary in response. It is a simple way to speak up about what matters to you.

Past Relationships

Reflect on how a past partner might have reacted to different situations. Choose the most likely response from four options and optionally explain your choice.

Compatibility Check

Choose one answer to each relationship scenario. After you answer, the second player decides compatible or not compatible, helping you discover where you align.

Goal Building

Type short goal entries in response to relationship prompts and build a shared vision for the future, one round at a time.

First Date Questions

Answer each question by choosing one of four options and optionally explaining your choice, with prompts ranging from easy icebreakers to deeper personal choices.

Pillow Talk

Quiet, open-ended prompts designed for honest late-night conversation. Type your full thought, save a screenshot, and share it when you are ready.

Romantic Questions

Rate intimate relationship questions on a feeling scale from 1 to 4. It is a low-pressure, high-connection experience for a slow evening together.

Date Night Ideas

Explore a curated list of date ideas and vote Accept or Decline on each one—a fun way to plan your next outing without endless back-and-forth.

Ice Breakers

Conversation starters designed to warm up any group quickly. Choose to use or skip each prompt during the first few minutes of any gathering.

Let’s Play Lab

11 party games and puzzles for groups.

Truth Or Dare

Choose Truth or Dare. Respond honestly to truths or admit a fib, and accept or decline dares—the choice is always yours.

Would You Rather

Choose one of two options, then type a short reason for your decision. Compare answers and discover whether the other players think like you.

Never Have I Ever

Read each prompt and select everything that applies to you. The more you have done, the more interesting the conversation becomes.

Act It Out Cinema

Act out iconic moments from popular films using up to eight clue lines revealed one at a time. The group shouts guesses, and the fewest hints wins.

Bingo Generator

A digital Bingo caller for any group size. Draw balls, confirm a Bingo, enter the winner’s name, and review the full session.

Eye Spy

Nine items are generated for you to find. Tap each item as you spot it in the room around you, then finish whenever you are ready.

Hot Potato Eliminator

Add player names and a prize, press Start, and watch the countdown eliminate players one by one until exactly one winner remains.

Lucky Draw

Enter all participant names, add a prize description, and draw a winner with a fast random selection.

Sudoku

Choose a 4×4, 6×6, or 9×9 puzzle and fill every row, column, and region without repeats. Notes, mistake checks, and up to three hints help when you get stuck.

Sliding Picture Puzzle

Choose one of 13 pictures and an Easy, Medium, or Hard grid. Slide adjacent tiles into the empty space, track your moves and time, preview the picture, or request a move guide.

Word Search

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard, then find words running horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Select each word in a straight line and use up to three first-letter hints.

Mystery Lab

9 wildcard and trivia games for any group.

Magic 8 Ball

Ask your burning question and reveal the Magic 8 Ball’s verdict. Whatever it says, it is probably right. Probably.

Random Facts

Explore 10 interesting facts per session for trivia warm-ups, conversation starters, or settling that debate no one can quite remember.

Riddles

Read each riddle and try to solve it. Reveal the answer when you are ready, then continue to the next challenge.

Hollywood Stars

Explore 10 Hollywood and movie facts per session, made for film fans, trivia warm-ups, and conversation starters.

Animal Kingdom

Explore 10 animal facts per session, made for animal lovers, trivia warm-ups, and conversation starters.

Animal Kingdom Trivia

Answer multiple-choice animal trivia questions, test what your group knows, and see who comes out on top.

Sports History

Explore 10 sports history facts per session for sports fans, trivia warm-ups, and conversation starters.

Sports History Quiz

Take on classic sports trivia covering major leagues and unforgettable moments, built for fans who are confident they know the game.

Mad Libs

Fill in 10 words—Person, Place, Animal, Object, Food, Adjective, Verb, Body Part, Vehicle, and Emotion—then watch a random story come to life.

About Someone Plays

Someone Plays is an independent game studio focused on turning ordinary moments into reasons to laugh, talk, and connect. Our games are designed for couples, friends, families, and groups who want meaningful interaction without complicated rules or constant internet access.

Let’s Play A Game began with a simple idea: one app should offer enough variety to fit date nights, parties, quiet conversations, road trips, and spontaneous gatherings. Each game is built to be approachable, replayable, and easy to share with the people already in the room.

The project is independently created and continually improved with care, creativity, and feedback from players. Someone Plays is committed to making inclusive entertainment that respects players’ privacy while helping people create memorable moments together.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

Our Commitment

Someone Plays respects your privacy and designs Let’s Play A Game to work without requiring an account or collecting personal gameplay information.

Information the App Does Not Collect

The app does not require or collect names, account credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or gameplay responses. Someone Plays does not sell or rent personal information.

Gameplay and Device Storage

Gameplay takes place on your device. Answers, player names, selections, and session activity are not transmitted to Someone Plays. Information may remain temporarily on your device as needed for the active game and is controlled by the app and your device.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Let’s Play A Game does not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, or cross-app tracking. The app does not use analytics tools to create profiles of individual players.

Mobile Store and Platform Services

The mobile store and device platform may process information related to purchases, downloads, crash reporting, or use of their services under their own privacy policies. Someone Plays does not control those independent platform practices.

Website Support Messages

If you voluntarily submit the Support form, the message you enter, along with limited technical information automatically processed by the form-delivery provider such as an IP address or submission time, may be transmitted so the request can be delivered and protected from spam. Do not include passwords, payment information, health information, or other sensitive personal details.

How Support Information Is Used

Support submissions are used only to review technical issues, respond to feedback when contact information is available, improve the app, prevent abuse, and maintain service records. Messages are not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Retention and Security

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to address the submission, maintain business records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

Let’s Play A Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should submit any support request concerning a child and should avoid including the child’s personal information.

Your Choices

You may use the app without submitting a support message. Because the support form does not request an email address, Someone Plays may be unable to identify you, reply directly, or locate a previous anonymous submission.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, website, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
